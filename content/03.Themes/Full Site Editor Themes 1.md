Gutenberg editor allows you to add blocks to your content and customize blogs. with FSE you can edit the header and the footer to edit the full page.  Modern block themes will allow you to enter the FSE under appearance when you have an appropriate theme selected. 

### Styles 
Styles is the first option and you have the option to preview changes as you make them by clicking the view button. It will allow you to customize each individual sections font and colors. 

### Navigation 
The navigation section allows you to setup the navigation bar for your sites this section can include external links, post, pages or a search bar and other icons such as social media links. inn the navigation setting you can have multiple navigation sections and place them freely on the site by choosing a sidebar or top or bottom navigation block. You can move the navigation buttons in to separate menus and place them in any order. 

### Pages
This section lists all pages on your site. Selecting a page allows you to edit its content directly within a live preview of your site's layout, headers, and footers. To adjust the structural appearance or layout of the page, you would edit its assigned template. You can arrange the pages in any order. 

### Templates
Templates are different layouts within the theme or you can create your own template or create individual template parts that can be used throughout templates as the name implies. A part of the template for instance would be the header/footer sections that aren't able to be edited on non FSE enabled themes. Each theme is made of templates and for each type of page, post or homepage. 

### Header/Footer
The header is usually consistent information shown across the top of the pages throughout the website, such as links to social media and the navigation menu. Though the header and navigation are different items on your site, they are often used interchangeably because the header contains the navigation menu.  The biggest difference between the header and footer is their location, header is at the top footer is at the bottom.
