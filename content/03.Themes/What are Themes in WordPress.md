[[What are Themes in WordPress]]

A theme controls the appearance of your WordPress site. It controls the colors, font, and overall layout. It is similar to applying Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) on an HTML site, but a theme is more complex than a simple HTML and CSS static site.

Themes can be altered at any time without changing the content, such as words and images placed on the site. The content is safe even if the theme breaks at some point.

Themes are part of what makes WordPress a popular content manager because they are accessible. They can be built custom by developers, downloaded for free from the official WordPress.org directory, or chosen from third-party marketplace sites that sell premium designs.

No matter the source, a good theme ensures your site's main structure, like headers, footers, and sidebars, looks professional and works across all devices.
