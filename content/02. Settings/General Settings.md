[[General Settings]]

At the bottom left of the sidebar you will see the settings here you can edit your title, tagline and other settings for your site. This will control the presence  of your site on the internet it is the first opportunity to control Search Engine Optimization(SEO). Though the tagline can be hidden it is an important part of SEO. You can select the time zone and time/date settings. 
**Reading**, is where you decide what your visitors will see on your site you can choose from your most recent post or a static introductory site. This setting allows the site to be used for more than a blog site. 
In the writing setting you can choose the default post and setup posting remotely from email and manage update services that allow you to notify followers on multiple sites that an update has happened. A holdover from when Wordpress was primarily a blogging website it can be completely disabled if your site is not a blogging site. Here I should note WP has outgrown it's start as a blogging site and has plugins that can make it a forum, ecommerce, wiki and informational sites. There are some limitations to the types of sites WP can create. The biggest types of sites that are incompatible are large enterprise stores, sites that require live interaction, or sites that require heavy security like financial or healthcare information.  You can also create a privacy policy for your website.
