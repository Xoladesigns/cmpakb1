### Writing 
First to add categories you would need to select post > categories to add categories to your website. If you have a specific website such as a food blog having the default category be *recipes* might be what you select. 

Under post format you will see several options these options are based on the theme installed. Options such as image, video and audio. 

You will also find that you can publish from email write a post from your email usch as your everyday GMail. You will be provided a secure email from wordpress and any email you send to it will be posted as a blog post. Your wordpress email should remain a secret because any emails received will be posted as blogs. There are also plugins that can accomplish this just in case wordpress removes it at a later date. 

Here you will manage your notification or update services that will let your followers know you have updated your blog. a popular plugin is pingomatic

#### Homepage Display 
With WP you have the option of a static homepage which could be an about me page an introduction to a blog. landing page, a search page, a profile page or any number of things. Each option can change with the template selected

You can also choose the number of most recent post you want to be posted this is useful to increase engagement without overwhelming visitors and keep content relevant.

You will also see syndication options that allow you to show how many post will show on sites other than your main webpage such as RSS feed, or social media sites.  You can choose if you would like the full blog or a scrollable excerpt to give the visitor options.
