There are built in tools on the dashboard that will help you manage your site. 

Import allows you to bring your content from other sites that allow it such as *blogger*, *live journal* *tumblr* and other popular sites including other wordpress sites. 

Export allows you to take the content from your site to other sites such as or if you want to move your content to a new host or domain. 

Site health: This allows you to manage plugins that are out of date. and see if anything is wrong with your site such as an outdated them updates to wordpress. 

You can also export/import personal data to comply with **CCPA** and **GDOR** privacy laws  

Erase Personal Data, will clear out all personal data
