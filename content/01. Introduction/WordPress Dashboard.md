---
title: The dashboard
description: An overview of the WP Dashboard
---
At the top there is the admin bar which can quickly take you to the front end of your site. Or you can have a shortcut to the creation of post, pages and media. Beyond these shortcuts the WP dashboard is the central command center for managing the backend of the entire website. You can customize the apparence using themes, install and manage plugins and manage user roles and permissions.  
WP originally started as a blogging platform and is still a primary function at managing that content and comments associated with blogs. The more upgraded dashboard has has more complex control over your site's backend structure. You can also personalize your view using the dashboard and manage all of the settings. You change reading preferences, permalinks this is the control center for your wordpress site.
