Wordpress is an open source content manager it allows you to build your websites and powers 36% percent of all websites on the internet and 63% of websites use WordPress for their content management sites.  

Wordpress.org is the software used to manage content on your website and build the website is free and opensource and gives total control over content. It does require more of a learning curve but unlike Wix, Squarespace and wordpress.com you can build whatever you want can't be banned or deleted and does not have a monthly fee to use.

WordPress.com is a similar platform to Wix and Squarepace with more freedom and the option to move the site to other host while keeping your content intact. It uses a similar drag and drop structure as competitors but not on as rigid of a grid system.  The biggest difference is that the dotcom site provides web hosting as well, which is paid.
