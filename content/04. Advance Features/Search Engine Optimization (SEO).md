---
title: Search Engine Optimization
desctiption: What is SEO, How to use it.
---
SEO is how your website ranks when people search for something your website references. The goal of SEO is to get more visitors to your site free and organically. There is a setting to make sure that the site is visible to search engines. Without paying, it may take time for your website to get noticed by searches. Google has released some guidelines on what its search is looking for and provided tips on SEO.

#### Fresh Content

Ensure that you are consistent in posting and updating your website, no matter if it is a business site, e-commerce site, or blog site. Search engines look for fresh, consistent content. Update your home page and other pages regularly, and make sure your plugins and themes are updated, along with your version of WP.

#### Quality Content

Google published the quality guideline of E.A.T: Expertise, Authority, and Trustworthiness. Here is where having an accurate, well-written "About Me" section will come in handy. Even if visitors don't read it, the search engine does.

#### Expertise

Write what you know and love. AI slop is easily identifiable by search engines and will not benefit you.

#### Authority

Authority comes from linking your site to your social media and being more visible. Link to reputable sites, and if your content is shared by popular authoritative people, that can help. If you have a large following on one platform like TikTok, you can link your website to get viewers there.

#### Trustworthiness

Make sure that the content you post is accurate and factual, another reason it might be useful to avoid AI to prevent plausible-sounding nonsense.

#### Keywords

Keywords are the terms people use that relate to your site. There are tools like [Wordstream](https://www.wordstream.com/) that help you know which keywords are currently popular and could be linked to your site.

#### Linking

Linking, even to your own site, will help with SEO because the more times a site is linked, the more important it appears to search engines. For example, if you have a site with recipes and you share a recipe for an entrée, link to a dessert recipe also on your site.

#### Metadata

Metadata is the hidden information on your site, such as tags on your posts. WP automatically creates metadata. Though plugins can allow you to edit this data, it is usually taken from the top portion of your content.

#### Site Quality

WP is a high-quality platform with editors that are relatively simple to use and themes with responsiveness built in. Choosing a host that has a reputation for speed is important. A caching plugin might help if your host doesn't have caching built in, which makes your site load faster. Ensure your site is updated and is only running needed plugins and updated themes. It is also recommended to create a sitemap for SEO. While it won't automatically boost SEO, a high-quality site is easier for search engines to locate.

#### Image Optimization

Make sure your images are scaled properly so that they don't take a long time to load when resized by the browser. Large images still take up space on your server and slow down your website. For accessibility and SEO, be sure to name your images accurately.
